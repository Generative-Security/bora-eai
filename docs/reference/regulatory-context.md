# Regulatory context

Dates and obligations that affect BORA-EAI assessments, as checked in November 2026. Law changes; check before relying on any date. This page is not legal advice.

## Australia

| Item | Date | What it means for AI use cases | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024: statutory tort for serious invasions of privacy | Commenced by 10 June 2025 | Individuals can sue for serious privacy invasions; relevant to B2, B3, B13 | `[secondary]` [MinterEllison](https://www.minterellison.com/articles/privacy-and-other-legislation-amendment-act-2024-now-in-effect) |
| Same Act: OAIC infringement and compliance-notice powers; "reasonable steps" include technical and organizational measures | 11 December 2024 | Raises enforcement exposure for AI data handling | `[secondary]` |
| Same Act: automated decision-making disclosure in privacy policies | **10 December 2026** | Use cases that make or support decisions significantly affecting individuals must be disclosed | `[secondary]` |
| APRA letter to industry on AI | 30 April 2026 | AI is "not subject to separate governance requirements"; managed under CPS 230 and CPS 234; flags vendor concentration and fourth-party risk; expects board AI education | `[secondary]` [HWL Ebsworth](https://hwlebsworth.com.au/not-so-fast-apra-issues-ai-reminder-ahead-of-cps-230-deadline/) |
| APRA CPS 230 (operational risk) applies to all contracted service providers | **1 July 2026** | AI vendors and their upstream dependencies fall within operational-risk management for regulated entities | `[secondary]` |
| Policy for the responsible use of AI in government, v2.0 (DTA) | 15 December 2025 | Agencies need an accountable officer per use case, a use-case register, and AI impact assessments | `[government]` [DTA](https://www.dta.gov.au/media-releases/ai-policy-overhauled-new-impact-assessment-tool-and-procurement-guidance) |
| Guidance for AI Adoption ("AI6") | Released October 2025; implementation guidance updated 2026 | Six voluntary practices: decide who is accountable; understand impacts and plan accordingly; measure and manage risks; share essential information; test and monitor; maintain human control. Replaces the Voluntary AI Safety Standard | `[secondary]` [Actuaries Institute](https://www.actuaries.asn.au/research-analysis/understanding-australia-s-ai6-a-framework-for-ai-governance) |
| Australia–US CLOUD Act Agreement | Signed 15 December 2021; reported in force from early 2024 | US may not target Australian persons under the agreement; narrows but does not remove US legal reach | `[government]` [Home Affairs](https://www.homeaffairs.gov.au/about-us/our-portfolios/national-security/lawful-access-telecommunications/australia-united-states-cloud-act-agreement) |
| Australian Consumer Law (misleading or deceptive conduct) | Ongoing | Applies in principle to what customer-facing AI says (B8). No chatbot-specific ACCC action found as of November 2026 | n/a |

## European Union

| Item | Date | What it means | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU AI Act penalties (Article 99) | In force | Up to €35 million or 7% of worldwide turnover for prohibited practices; up to €15 million or 3% for most other operator obligations | `[government]` [AI Act Service Desk](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-99) |
| Article 50 transparency (including telling people they are interacting with AI) | **2 August 2026** (not deferred) | Customer-facing AI must be disclosed (B8, B10) | `[secondary]` [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/) |
| Article 50(2) marking of AI-generated content: grace period for systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 | **2 December 2026** | Generative output marking for older systems | `[secondary]` [White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/eu-ai-omnibus-enters-force-amending-ai-act) |
| Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 | Published 24 July 2026; in force 27 July 2026 | Defers high-risk obligations; softens AI literacy to "support"; adds new prohibitions; gives the AI Office new enforcement tools | `[secondary]` White & Case |
| High-risk obligations, Annex III (for example, employment, credit, education) | **2 December 2027** (deferred from 2 August 2026) | Optional BORA-EAI override to High tier | `[secondary]` |
| High-risk obligations, Annex I (AI in regulated products) | **2 August 2028** (deferred from 2 August 2027) | Product-embedded AI | `[secondary]` |
| GDPR Chapter V transfers; EU–US Data Privacy Framework status; EU Data Act | Not verified in this draft | Relevant to B13 for EU data | n/a |
