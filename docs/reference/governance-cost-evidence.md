# Governance cost evidence

Supporting reference for [Layer 8](../layers/L8-governance-cost.md) and [B15](../scenarios/B15-governance-cost-and-failure.md). Hard data on the cost of governing AI is scarce. Most figures below come from sellers of the relevant service or from estimates made before the final law existed.

## ISO/IEC 42001 certification

`[vendor]` [ISMS.online](https://www.isms.online/iso-42001/certification/certification-cost/), a seller of compliance tooling:

| Organization size | Total cost | Audit days |
|---|---|---|
| 1–50 staff | £8,000–£15,000 | 3–5 |
| 51–250 staff | £15,000–£30,000 | 5–10 |
| 250+ staff | £30,000–£50,000+ | 10–20 |

- Stage 1 audit £2,000–6,000; Stage 2 £3,000–15,000 (together 30–40% of the total).
- Internal implementation £3,000–15,000 over 3–9 months.
- Consultants £800–1,500 a day for 5–15 days (optional).
- Compliance platform £5,000–15,000 a year (optional).
- Surveillance audits £1,500–5,000 a year; recertification every three years.
- Existing ISO 27001 certification saves an estimated 30–40% of implementation cost.

Other sellers publish similar estimates (for example, Vanta, Sprinto).

## EU AI Act compliance

All of these were estimated on the 2021 proposal, before the final 2024 text and the 2026 Digital Omnibus. Use them as orders of magnitude only.

| Source | Estimate | Label |
|---|---|---|
| European Commission impact assessment, SWD(2021) 84 | Supplying an average high-risk system (~€170,000): ~€6,000–7,000 compliance; human oversight ~€5,000–8,000 a year; verification ~€3,000–7,500; aggregate ~€100–500 million by 2025 | `[government]` `[not re-checked]` ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52021SC0084)) |
| Supporting study (CEPS et al.) | ~€29,277 a year per AI unit (~17% of development cost) | `[government-commissioned]` `[not re-checked]` |
| CEPS (Laurer & Renda, 2021) | Quality management system setup ~€193,000–330,000, plus ~€71,400 a year, for firms without one; argued per-system figures were overstated | `[think tank]` `[not re-checked]` ([CEPS](https://www.ceps.eu/clarifying-the-costs-for-the-eus-ai-act/)) |
| ITIF / Center for Data Innovation (2021) | €31 billion over five years; up to €400,000 for an SME deploying one high-risk system; disputed by CEPS | `[think tank, industry-aligned]` ([ITIF](https://itif.org/publications/2021/07/26/how-much-will-artificial-intelligence-act-cost-europe/)) |

## Tooling market

Forrester (late 2024) projected AI governance software spending of about US$15.8 billion by 2030; Gartner has published AI governance platform forecasts. Neither was verified for this draft `[analyst]` `[unverified]`.

## Not found

- Verified data on AI governance team size or budget per unit of revenue or per AI system.
- Reliable public pricing for AI red teaming or LLM security assessments.
- Measured (post-2024) EU AI Act compliance costs.
- Any study of whether governance spend raises the rate at which AI projects reach production or deliver value.

These are the most useful gaps for contributors to fill.
