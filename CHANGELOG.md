## 0.1 · November 2026 · Working draft for review

First public draft.

- Nine layers, each answering one executive question.
- Two lenses: internal and customer-facing.
- Fifteen business threat scenarios (B1 to B15), each with one accountable owner.
- Tiering: fast lane, Low, Medium, and High, with two override rules.
- Range-based net position per use case, with tail loss.
- Supporting material: landscape review, framework crosswalk, sovereignty reference, regulatory context, incident library, value and governance-cost evidence, glossary, and references.
- Templates: use-case register, assessment, scenario register, fast-lane patterns, sovereignty profile, and board summary.
- Worked example: an illustrative customer-service chatbot.
- Validation plan and open questions.
